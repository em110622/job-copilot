# Stories

Your interview story bank. Each story is the long, spoken telling of one or more forløb in `experience.md`. The facts live in the forløb; the story adds the tension, the decisions, and what you learned. If a story and its forløb ever disagree, the forløb wins, and the co-pilot flags the difference.

Add stories as they come up (experience-coach, mock-interview, and interview-debrief all propose new ones).

## Format

```
### <short story title>
- Built on forløb: <title(s) in experience.md>
- Answers questions like: <e.g. leading without authority, a conflict, a failure, delivering under pressure, building something from scratch>
- Situation: <the context, two lines>
- Task: <what was at stake, and what was yours to do>
- Action: <what you did, the key decisions, in first person>
- Result: <the outcome, using only results from the forløb; respect publicity levels>
- Learning: <what you would do the same or differently>
- Length: <how long it runs out loud, aim for about two minutes>
- Practice notes: <feedback from mock interviews and real ones>
```
