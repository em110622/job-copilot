# Decisions

An append-only log of decisions in your search, so Claude does not re-litigate settled questions and can remind you why you chose what you chose (e.g. declined a role, dropped a target, chose a positioning, set a salary floor).

Format, newest at the top:

```
[YYYY-MM-DD] Decision: what was decided.
Why: the reason.
Alternatives considered: what you passed on, and why.
```
