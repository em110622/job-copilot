Captures who you met, what they asked, how your answers landed, signals about fit and next steps; updates your story bank, contacts, and the application status; and drafts a thank-you note for you to send. Triggers on "debrief", "I just had my interview", "interview went", "log my interview".
---

## GUIDED DELIVERY - one part at a time (always)
(1) capture, (2) what it means, (3) updates to memory, (4) thank-you draft. Wait for the user between parts.

---

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `memory/contacts.md`, `memory/stories.md`, and the application folder (`posting.md`, `research.md`, earlier debriefs) first.

## Part 1 - Capture (let the user dump, then fill gaps)
Invite a free-form dump (typing or dictation is fine). Then ask only what is missing:
- Who did you meet (names, roles)? Anything notable about each?
- Which questions were asked? Which answers felt strong, which weak?
- What did you learn about the role, team, or company that wasn't in the posting?
- Signals: their enthusiasm, concerns raised, compensation or timing mentioned.
- Next steps they stated, and by when.
- Anything you promised to send or do?

## Part 2 - What it means
Three to six bullets: how it likely went and why, concerns to address in the next round or the thank-you note, whether the role still fits `memory/targets.md`, and what to prepare next.

## Part 3 - Memory updates (propose, write on yes)
- `debrief-YYYY-MM-DD.md` in the application folder with the capture and Part 2.
- `posting.md`: status and `Next step:` / `Follow up by:` from what they said (or the default in `memory/tools.md`).
- `memory/contacts.md`: each interviewer under "Interviewers met", with what was promised by whom.
- `memory/stories.md`: practice notes on stories used; new questions added to the question map; a new story if one emerged.
- `memory/experience.md`: any new fact about a forløb the user mentioned (hand to `experience-coach` if it needs shaping).

## Part 4 - Thank-you note
Draft a short thank-you per interviewer (or one to the main contact, matching the market's norms): specific to something discussed, addressing one concern if useful, in the user's voice and the interview's language. Draft-only. If the user asks, save it as an email draft in the tool from `memory/tools.md`; never send.
