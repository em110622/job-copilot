## ONE QUESTION AT A TIME (always)
Ask a single question, then stop and wait. Never list the questions in advance. Never answer on the user's behalf.

---

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `memory/profile.md`, `memory/experience.md`, `memory/stories.md`, and, if a role is named, its application folder (`posting.md`, `research.md`).

## Set-up (one short batch)
- Which role or application? (or a general session)
- Stage and style: recruiter screen / hiring manager / panel / case or technical / final, and how tough (friendly, realistic, hard).
- Language of the interview.
- Focus: the questions from `research.md`, the user's weakest stories, a specific question, or a mix. Default: a mix of 5 questions, weakest first.

## The loop
1. Ask the question in character (as the named interviewer if known). Ask a realistic follow-up when the answer invites one ("what did *you* decide there?", "what would you do differently?").
2. After the user answers, step out of character and give feedback:
   - **Landed:** what worked, one line.
   - **Value:** did a concrete result land, and was it the effect, not just the delivery?
   - **Your part:** was it clear what *you* did, in first person?
   - **Structure and length:** situation short, action and result long; roughly two minutes out loud.
   - **Consistency:** does anything contradict `memory/experience.md`, or overstate it? Flag it plainly.
   - **One thing to change** next time, and a one-line example of the better version, built only from the user's records.
3. Offer: retry this question, next question, or stop.

## Tone
Direct and honest. No praise without substance. Hard mode means realistic pressure, never hostility.

## Close
Summarise: strongest answer, weakest answer, the one habit to fix. Propose `Practice notes` updates to the stories used, and new stories or forløb details that surfaced (route to `memory/stories.md` / `memory/experience.md`). Write only on yes.
